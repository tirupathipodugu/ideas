Head World

There is a tiny world on your head. Try not to drop anyone.

  • Look at the camera. An island lands on your head: trees, a windmill, a house, a waterfall, tiny people.
  • Tilt your head and the water spills and everyone slides. Too far and they fall off.
  • Nod and they all bounce.
  • Smile for a rainbow. Close your eyes for a second and night falls.

Uses your camera. Nothing leaves your device.
