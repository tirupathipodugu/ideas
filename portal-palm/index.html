Portal Palm

There is a hole in your hand. Something lives down there.

  • Open your hand to the camera and hold it still. A portal opens in your palm.
  • Wait. One of them peeks out first. Then the rest come.
  • Make a fist and the portal sucks every one of them back in.
  • Open both hands for two portals.
