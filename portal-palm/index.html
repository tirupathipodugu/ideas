Portal Palm

There is a hole in your hand. Something lives down there.

  • Open your hand, palm to the camera, and hold it still. A portal opens right in your palm.
  • One peeks out first. Then they keep climbing out for as long as your hand stays open.
  • Move and they drift with you. Wave your hand through them and they get swept away.
  • Make a fist to shut the portal. They stay. Open again for more.
  • Point a finger and they swarm it, circling your fingertip.
  • Open both hands and they leap from one palm into the other.
  • Two fists suck every one of them back in.
  • Open both hands for two portals.

Uses your camera. Nothing leaves your device.
