Doodle Alive

Draw anything. Hold it up. Watch it walk off the page.

  • ✏️ Draw with a dark pen or marker on plain paper
  • 📄 Hold it inside the box and tap Bring to life
  • 🧗 It climbs onto your head and shoulders, and hides behind you
  • 👆 Grab it and throw it, poke it, or swat it with your hand

Uses your camera. Everything stays on your device; nothing is uploaded.
