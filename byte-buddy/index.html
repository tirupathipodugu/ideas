Byte

A tiny buddy that lives on your shoulder.

  • Open one hand and a sparkle appears. Byte jumps to catch it and rides your palm.
  • Open both hands and Byte splits into 100 coloured clones that rush to you.
  • Close your hand and Byte jumps back to your shoulder.
  • Hold your arm out flat: Byte walks along it. Point it down and Byte slides.
  • Say "come here", "go back", "climb", "peek", "hang", "slide", "dance", "wave", "hide", "multiply".

Uses your camera and microphone. Nothing leaves your device.
