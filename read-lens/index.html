Read Lens

Read your paper book faster.

Hold your phone over the page. Live explains the lines you're on as you read, a glowing pacer runs along the printed lines at your speed, Focus dims everything but your line, and Skim lights up the lines that matter.

Free, no sign-up. The page is read on your phone.
