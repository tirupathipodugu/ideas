Mini Me

A tiny you, on your shoulder. It copies you. Mostly.

  • Step back so your face and both shoulders are in view.
  • It wears your face, live, half a second late, and copies your arms.
  • Pull a silly face. It remembers, and plays it back later.
  • Bring your hand close for a high five.

Uses your camera. Your face never leaves your device.
