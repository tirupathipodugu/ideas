Stretch Wall

Been sitting too long? Here come the walls.

  • Each wall has a hole shaped like a stretch
  • Get into the shape before it reaches you, then hold it
  • Lose the pose and the wall breaks. Three breaks and you're done

Works sitting at your desk. Sit back a little so your head, shoulders and arms are in view.

Go gently, and skip or stop anything that hurts. This is a game, not medical advice.

Uses your camera. Everything stays on your device; nothing is uploaded or saved.
