Pet Cloud

A tiny cloud that lives above your hand and feels what you feel.

  • Open your hand. Your cloud floats above it.
  • Smile and it throws a rainbow. Frown and it rains on your palm.
  • Scowl for a thunderstorm. Clap for a lightning strike.
  • Make a fist under it to wring it out. Blow at it. Poke it.

Uses your camera. Nothing leaves your device.
